## Caden Holland

I build software for small local service businesses in the Tulsa, Oklahoma
area, through my company Holland Tech: free tools an owner can use today, and
small fixed-price builds for the work that eats their week. Most of what I ship
runs on Cloudflare (Pages, Workers, D1) with Supabase or Postgres behind it,
written in TypeScript and Python.

What I've built, what it's for, and what state it's in:
